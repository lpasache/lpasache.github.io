About Me

Born in Peru to a Chinese-immigrant father and a Peruvian mother, I developed a global perspective and a resilient work ethic early on. I am the middle child of three siblings and began my academic journey with two years of Civil Engineering at the Pontificia Universidad Católica del Perú (PUCP). While working part-time at a sushi bar to support my studies, I met my husband and made the life-changing decision to relocate to Los Angeles.

Arriving in California, my biggest hurdle wasn't the engineering concepts—it was overcoming a deep shyness when speaking English as a Second Language (ESL). To build my confidence, I enrolled in ESL classes at Downey Adult School (DAS). Once I received my green card, I stepped directly into the fast-paced environment of Panera Bread. Starting as a prep-cook and line cook, I eventually pushed myself into a cashier role specifically to force myself out of my comfort zone. Engaging with hundreds of customers daily completely broke me out of my shell and solidified my communication skills.

With newfound confidence, I took the assessment exams at East Los Angeles College (ELAC), placing directly into Calculus I and college-level English. This marked the launchpad for my American engineering journey: I earned three Associate degrees from ELAC, transferring in 2021 to University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA), where I graduated with my B.S. in Mechanical Engineering in 2024. In 2021, I also proudly became a U.S. citizen, unlocking the ability to contribute to the nation's most critical aerospace initiatives. Currently, I am pursuing my M.S. in Mechanical Engineering at Cal Poly Pomona (CPP) with an expected graduation in December 2026. My professional path has since evolved from high-volume operations to cutting-edge aerospace engineering, including an engineering internship at Performance Team (Maersk) and my current role as a Graduate Intern at NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL). I leverage my multicultural background, operational grit, and passion for complex problem-solving to design and optimize next-generation mechanical systems.
